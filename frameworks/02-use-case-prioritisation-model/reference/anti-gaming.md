# Anti-gaming provisions

Any scoring model that allocates budget will be gamed. These provisions are not cynicism; they are what makes the output trustworthy enough to be worth producing.

1. **The sponsor presents; the panel scores.** Non-negotiable.
2. **Every score above 3 requires stated evidence.** No evidence, score capped at 3.
3. **The rubric is frozen between cycles.** Changing weights mid-cycle, after seeing where candidates land, is the most common corruption of the process. Weight changes take effect at the next cycle, are proposed in writing, and are minuted.
4. **Do not re-score from scratch each cycle.** Carry scores forward and record deltas with reasons. Wholesale re-scoring imports the current political weather into the numbers.
5. **Publish the whole portfolio, including the refusals.** A model whose rejections are invisible will be routed around.
6. **Record the exception log.** Every override of the model, with its reason and its owner.
7. **Calibrate annually.** Compare predicted against realised value for completed use cases. Where the model was wrong, fix the rubric — not the outcome.
