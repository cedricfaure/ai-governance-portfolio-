# Panel session agenda

## Pre-reads (circulate at least 3 working days before the session)

- The AI Use Case Business Card for every candidate on the agenda
- The current portfolio register, including scores carried forward from the last cycle
- Any proposed rubric or weight changes — for information only; they take effect next cycle, not this one, per the anti-gaming rule against mid-cycle changes

## Panel composition

Minimum: business sponsor (presents, does not score), process owner, data lead, technology lead, risk and compliance. Confirm attendance before the session is scheduled — a panel missing risk and compliance, or data, is not quorate.

## Timeboxing per candidate

- Sponsor presents: 10 minutes, cards only, no slides beyond the Business Card itself
- Panel questions: 10 minutes
- Sponsor withdraws
- Panel scores and discusses evidence: 15 minutes
- **New candidate:** score every criterion from the rubric, in full
- **Returning candidate:** carry forward the prior scores; discuss and agree only the deltas, with reasons recorded

## The rule

The sponsor presents; the panel scores. This is not a courtesy — it is the control that keeps the exercise honest. The sponsor leaves the room (or the call) before scoring starts.

## Handling disagreement

Where panellists disagree on a score, do not average it away silently. Record the dispersion — the range of scores given and the reasons for it — against that criterion. A wide dispersion is itself information: it usually means the evidence grade should be lower than anyone individually proposed.

## What gets minuted

- The score and evidence grade agreed for every criterion, for every candidate
- The dispersion, where panellists disagreed
- Any exception raised against the model's output, with its reason and its owner
- Any proposed rubric or weight change, logged for the next cycle
- Attendance
