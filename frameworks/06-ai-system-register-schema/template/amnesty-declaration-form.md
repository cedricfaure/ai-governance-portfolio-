# Tell us what you are using

**This takes under five minutes. Nothing you tell us here will be withdrawn, and nobody will be disciplined for anything you declare in this window.** We want to see what is actually being used, not catch anyone out. If a tool is genuinely useful, telling us about it is the fastest way to keep using it properly.

We are asking about anything that helps you decide, sort, rank, score, draft or answer something, at work. That includes free tools, personal-account tools, browser add-ons, spreadsheets you built yourself, and anything switched on inside a product you already use.

## Six questions

**1. What is it?**
Name it however you know it. A link or a screenshot is fine if that is easier than a name.

**2. What do you use it for?**
One sentence. What does it help you decide, sort, rank, score, draft or answer?

**3. Does it touch information about specific customers, applicants, patients, employees or other people?**
Yes / No / Not sure

**4. Who else uses it, as far as you know?**
Just you, your team, or wider than that.

**5. How did you start using it?**
It came with something we already had, someone recommended it, you found it yourself, or something else.

**6. Is there anything about it you are unsure of or slightly uneasy about?**
Nothing is too small to mention here.

## What happens next

Everything declared in this window becomes an entry on the AI system register. Most declarations will simply be recorded and left as they are. A small number may lead to a short, friendly conversation if we need to understand more, never a sanction. We will publish what we found, in aggregate, and what we did about it.
