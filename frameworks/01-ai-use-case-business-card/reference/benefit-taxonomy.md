# Benefit taxonomy

## Value hypothesis

Classify each benefit by shape. Different shapes need different baselines, units and payback horizons — mixing them is the most common source of an indefensible number.

| # | Benefit shape | Unit | Baseline needed | Typical honesty risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Cost avoidance** | [CUR] not spent | Committed budget or signed plan | Cost was never actually coming |
| B2 | **Labour time compression** | Hours × fully-loaded rate | Time-motion or process mining | Hours freed but never redeployed |
| B3 | **Work deflection** | Volume handled without a human | Historical volume + containment rate | Deflected work returns as escalations |
| B4 | **Revenue generation** | Incremental [CUR] | Controlled experiment | Cannibalisation from other channels |
| B5 | **Loss / risk reduction** | Δ Annual Loss Expectancy | Historical incident and loss data | Counting the same loss twice |
| B6 | **Quality / error reduction** | Δ defect or rework rate | Historical error rate | Errors displaced, not eliminated |
| B7 | **Non-monetisable mission value** | Natural units (see the non-financial value section of the [KPI library](kpi-library.md)) | Outcome measure baseline | Used to justify a case that fails on B1–B6 |

**Rule on B2:** an hour saved is not money saved. The card must state the **realisation path**: redeployed to named higher-value work, absorbed into reduced overtime, avoided a planned hire, or **not realised** (state it). Unrealised time savings are recorded at zero financial value and reported as capacity, not cash.

**Rule on B7:** non-monetisable benefits are legitimate and must be recorded — but they cannot be the sole justification for a use case whose monetisable case is negative *unless* the sponsor is a public body pursuing a stated mission objective and the case is explicitly presented as such.
