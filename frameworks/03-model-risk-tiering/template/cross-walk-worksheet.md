# Cross-walk worksheet

**Note: this worksheet is deliberately not pre-filled with example regime
mappings.** This framework defines the mechanism; the authoritative control
mappings belong in the Cross-Regulation Control Library (a separate
framework in this portfolio). Until that exists, populate the table below
per organisation from primary sources. A wrong article reference is worse
than an empty field.

**Two rules govern how this worksheet relates to the internal tier:**

1. The internal tier may exceed the external classification. It may never
   fall below it.
2. Mitigation never lowers a regulatory classification. Controls reduce
   residual risk; they do not alter the legal character of the system. A
   system that is high-risk under an external regime carries those
   obligations regardless of how well controlled it is — the Residual Tier
   has no effect on regime-derived obligations.
